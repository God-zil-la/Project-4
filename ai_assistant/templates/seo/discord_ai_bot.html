Create a Custom Discord AI Bot

Create a custom Discord AI bot with your own instructions and knowledge. Connect your AI Assistant to Discord and support your server community.

An AI assistant for your Discord community

AI Assistant supports connecting a configured assistant to Discord. Create an assistant for a community, project or organization, then use the supported Discord integration to bring its responses into your server.

Build a bot with your own personality

Give the assistant its own name, instructions, behavior and subject focus. You can build a specialist assistant for a gaming community, a study group, a hobby club or a business server.

Use a Knowledge Base for server questions

Upload text-based PDF, DOCX or TXT documents or add manual information. The assistant can retrieve relevant knowledge when responding, making it useful for frequently asked questions and community reference material.

How to get started

Create an account, configure an assistant and follow the Discord connection setup available in the platform. The supported integration is available according to plan entitlements; check current plan details before subscribing.

What the integration does not promise

This page describes the existing Discord connection, not a general customer-facing API. Broader Discord integration improvements and API Access remain separate planned work.
