Build an AI Assistant with Your Own Knowledge

Give your AI assistant information that is relevant to you, your projects or your business. Add your own material to the Knowledge Base and use it with specialized AI assistants.

What is an AI Knowledge Base?

A Knowledge Base gives an AI assistant additional information to work with. Instead of relying only on a general-purpose assistant, you can provide material related to a specific subject, project or business.

Use your own documents and information

Add your own material so your assistant can use information that matters to the task you created it for. This can help you build assistants around specialized knowledge instead of treating every conversation the same.

Knowledge for personal projects and studies

Individuals can use a Knowledge Base for projects, research, studying and other work where their own information provides useful context for an AI assistant.

Knowledge for business AI assistants

Businesses can create assistants around company information, recurring questions and other relevant material. Different assistants can be created for different purposes while using knowledge appropriate to their role.

Use your knowledge with a website chatbot

Knowledge-enabled assistants can also be used with the Website Widget and Public Chatbot, allowing Pro users to make a specialized assistant available to website visitors.

Keep improving your assistants

Create assistants for different purposes, manage their conversations and update the knowledge they use as your needs change.
