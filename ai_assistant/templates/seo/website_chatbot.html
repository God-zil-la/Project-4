Add an AI Chatbot to Your Website

Create a custom AI chatbot and make it available directly on your website. Configure an assistant around your needs, add your own knowledge and give visitors a convenient way to interact with your assistant.

Create a chatbot for your website visitors

Build an assistant for the questions and information that matter to your visitors. A website chatbot can help people find information and interact with your business or project without leaving your website.

Give your chatbot your own knowledge

Add documents and information to the Knowledge Base so your assistant can use material that is relevant to your website, organization or business.

Embed your AI chatbot on your website

Pro users can use the Website Widget to embed an assistant directly on a website. The chatbot can also be shared through its public chat page when you want a standalone link.

Keep visitor conversations separate

Each public visitor gets a separate conversation with the selected assistant. Visitor chats are saved so the assistant owner can view them from the existing Conversations area.

Customize the assistant for its purpose

Create different assistants for different websites, projects or business needs and configure each one around the role it should perform.

Track chatbot activity

Use AI Assistant analytics to follow activity and understand how your assistants are being used.
