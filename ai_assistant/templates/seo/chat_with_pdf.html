Chat With Your PDF Documents

Chat with text-based PDF documents using a custom AI assistant. Upload knowledge, ask questions and retrieve relevant information from your files.

Ask questions about your own PDF content

AI Assistant lets you upload PDFs containing extractable text to a Knowledge Base. Your assistant can retrieve relevant passages to help answer questions about that information.

Useful for studies and projects

Use your own course material, manuals, guides or project documentation as context. Keep your document questions in conversations associated with the relevant assistant.

More than PDFs

The Knowledge Base also supports DOCX, TXT and manually entered text. Combining these sources helps create an assistant for a specific subject.

Important PDF limitation

Image-only and scanned PDFs without extractable text are not processed through OCR. For best results, use text-based PDF documents and verify important answers against the original source.
