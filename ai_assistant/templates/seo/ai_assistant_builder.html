Create a specialized assistant

AI Assistant lets you create assistants for different purposes rather than using a single generic chatbot. Name your assistant, choose a focus and manage its settings from your account.

Customize instructions and personality

Configure instructions, personality and behavior to suit your work, studies, community or personal projects. A dedicated assistant can keep a consistent focus on the information you want it to use.

Add your documents and knowledge

Extend your assistant with text-based PDF, DOCX, TXT and manually entered knowledge. Relevant information can be retrieved when you ask questions.

Keep conversations separate

Each assistant can have its own conversations, allowing you to return to earlier discussions without mixing unrelated projects.
