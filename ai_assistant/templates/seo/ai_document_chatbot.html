Create an AI Chatbot With Your Documents

Create an AI document chatbot with PDF, DOCX, TXT and your own text. Build a knowledge-powered assistant for research, projects or business.

Turn your information into assistant knowledge

A document chatbot can use your uploaded files as context for answers. AI Assistant supports text-based PDFs, Word DOCX files, TXT files and manually entered information.

Relevant retrieval, not just keyword matching

The Knowledge Base uses retrieval-augmented generation with exact, lexical and semantic matching strategies to find relevant information for a question.

Separate knowledge for different assistants

Create dedicated assistants for different projects or departments. Knowledge is associated with its owning assistant, rather than being shared indiscriminately between accounts.

Document chat for businesses

A business can use its own FAQs, product information or internal reference documents to create a specialist assistant. Pro users can also make a configured assistant available through the Website Widget.

Know the limits

Uploaded files must meet the supported format and storage limits. Scanned PDFs without text extraction are not supported by the current Knowledge processing pipeline.
