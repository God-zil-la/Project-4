Build a custom AI chatbot for personal use, projects, websites or business. Give your chatbot a purpose, add your own knowledge and keep its conversations organized in one place.

What can you use an AI chatbot for?

An AI chatbot can help answer questions, work with information, support personal projects and provide useful responses to customers or website visitors. You can create different assistants for different purposes instead of relying on one generic chatbot for every task.

Create a chatbot with your own knowledge

Add documents and information to the Knowledge Base so your chatbot can use material that is relevant to you, your project or your business.

AI chatbots for individuals

Create chatbots for studying, research, documents, personal projects or other specialized tasks. Keep separate assistants and conversations organized around what you are working on.

AI chatbots for businesses

Businesses can create specialized chatbots for customer questions, internal knowledge and other recurring information needs. Each assistant can be configured for its own purpose.

Add an AI chatbot to your website

Pro users can publish an assistant through a public link or embed it on a website with the Website Widget and Public Chatbot. Visitor conversations are kept with the selected assistant so they can be managed from AI Assistant.

Use your chatbot on Discord

AI Assistant also supports Discord integration, allowing you to connect an assistant and interact with it from Discord.
