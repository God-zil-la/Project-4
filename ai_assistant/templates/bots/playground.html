📚 What is Knowledge?

Knowledge lets you teach your AI Assistant about information that is important to you, your business, your products, your documents or anything else you want this bot to know.

Simply upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT file. Your assistant reads the content and adds it to this bot's knowledge.

When you later ask a question, the bot can use information from your uploaded documents to give you a more relevant and useful answer.

💡 Simple example

Imagine you create a customer support bot for your business.

You upload a document containing your products, prices, delivery information, return policy and frequently asked questions.

A user can then ask:

“How long does delivery take?”

Instead of relying only on general AI knowledge, your assistant can use the information you uploaded to answer the question.

📄 What can I upload?

  • Product and service information
  • FAQs and customer support information
  • Instructions and manuals
  • Company information
  • Policies and procedures
  • Guides and documentation
  • Notes and reference material

Supported file types: PDF, DOCX and TXT

🤖 Each bot can know different things

Knowledge is connected to the bot you upload it to.

For example, you could have one bot that knows everything about your products, another bot for technical support, and another bot that uses completely different documents.

Uploading or deleting knowledge from this bot does not change the knowledge used by your other bots.

🔄 You are in control

You can upload more documents whenever you want to give your assistant more information.

You can also delete uploaded knowledge when you no longer want this bot to use it.

🔒 Before you upload

Only upload information you have permission to use. Do not upload passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment details or other secrets.
