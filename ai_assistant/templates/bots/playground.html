📚 How does Knowledge Upload work?

Knowledge Upload lets you give {{ bot.name }} custom information that it can use when answering questions.

You can upload a TXT, PDF or DOCX document, or paste text manually. The content is processed and added to this bot's knowledge base.

Good examples

  • FAQs
  • Product information
  • Instructions and manuals
  • Company documentation
  • Policies and procedures
  • Guides and reference material
💡 Bot-specific knowledge

Knowledge uploaded here belongs to {{ bot.name }}. This means each of your bots can have its own documents and information.

🔒 Security

Only upload information you have permission to use. Never upload passwords, API keys, access tokens or other secrets.
