What is deleted

The confirmed online flow removes your account, profile, authentication/API tokens, assistants, conversations, chat messages (including legacy messages), Knowledge Base text and embeddings, usage records and local subscription/checkout records. It also removes locally stored billing emails addressed to your account email. Conversations belonging to your assistants are removed with those assistants.

Uploaded files belonging to your assistants or uploaded by you are queued for deletion from application storage after the database transaction succeeds. Cleanup is attempted immediately. If storage is unavailable, a retry record containing the file path remains until cleanup succeeds. A file still referenced by another Knowledge Base item is kept until that reference is removed. Deletion is permanent; account data cannot be recovered through the app.

Subscriptions and access

Online deletion first checks billing. Active subscriptions, pending changes, open checkout sessions or billing that cannot be verified must be resolved before automatic deletion. Use Billing or email support to request deletion and help ending billing. A subscription set to cancel at the end of a period is still active until it ends; contact support if you want deletion sooner. This flow does not automatically cancel subscriptions, charge you or issue refunds.

When Android subscriptions are enabled, manage them through Google Play subscriptions and include them in your support request. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription or delete an account. Deletion invalidates account access on other devices and the Discord/API connection, but does not erase messages already posted in Discord.

What may remain

A minimal follow-up record containing your email address, Stripe customer identifier (if any) and request date is retained for support to coordinate provider-side deletion and communicate any exceptions. Support removes it when that work is complete. File-cleanup jobs remain only until processed successfully. Neither queue currently has a fixed automatic completion deadline.

Stripe webhook event IDs and processing dates remain to prevent duplicate event processing; they contain no stored payment payload, and the application has no automatic expiry for them. The per-account rate-limit counter expires after 60 seconds if immediate cache cleanup is unavailable.

Provider-held information, delivered emails, Discord messages, hosting logs and backups are not erased by the application's database deletion. Support must review provider deletion and any legally required billing, security or fraud-prevention records. Their retention periods are not configured in this application. Support will explain any applicable exceptions when handling your request.

See the Privacy Policy's retention and deletion section for further details.
